No spoilers

While delayed or replaying, results, standings and the calendar winner for this race stay hidden, and nothing from "the future" is shown: flags, messages, points, celebrations.

Delay - match your TV / stream

s

Quick sync: press the button the moment the leader crosses the line on your stream.

With the real-time stream the delay is accurate to a second or two; on polling only, to about the poll interval.

Full replays - any session, any season

Replays run on a virtual clock - only what had happened by that moment is shown. Use the purple bar to scrub, pause and change speed.

Alerts

Pop-ups when something happens - timed to what you are seeing, so they respect your delay.

Test mode

Buttons that play the start lights, banners, map effects and celebrations on demand, using the session that's loaded. Only on this screen; the data isn't touched.

Layout
